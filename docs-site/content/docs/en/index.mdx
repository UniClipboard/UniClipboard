## Why UniClipboard

- **Local-first.** Sync runs over your LAN; clips never leave your network.
- **Cross-platform.** macOS, Windows, and Linux share the same clip stream.
- **Rich payloads.** Files, images, RTF, and HTML — not just plain text.
- **Keyboard-first.** A quick panel built for hands-on-keys flow.
