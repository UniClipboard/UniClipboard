title: Welcome to UniClipboard
description: Copy once, paste on every device. Cross-device clipboard sync with no account and no cloud.
---

UniClipboard (CLI `unic`) syncs your clipboard across your computers: copy on one device, paste directly on another.

- **Cross-platform**: Syncs across Windows, macOS, and Linux, over local Wi-Fi or across networks, plus a CLI tool.
- **No cloud**: Devices connect directly over encrypted links—no registration required, and history stays only on your own devices.
- **Lightweight**: The background sync service `unicd` uses about 14 MB of memory when idle (measured on macOS Apple Silicon with an empty history).
- **Rich content**: Text, images, files, and entire folders.

<Callout type="warn">
UniClipboard v2 has not published official installers yet; for now you can run it from source. See [Installation](./getting-started/install).
</Callout>

## Privacy Summary

- End-to-end encrypted between devices; UniClipboard runs no servers that store your clipboard content.
- Every new device requires you to verify its fingerprint and approve it; removed devices lose access immediately.
- Clipboard content, file paths, and device addresses are never written to logs.

For more details, see [Privacy & Security](./core-features/privacy-and-security).
