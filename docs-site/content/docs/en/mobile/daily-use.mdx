Once you're connected to a desktop (if you aren't yet, start with
[Connect to a Desktop](./connect)), you do almost everything from the
main screen: view history, upload content, and control syncing.

## Main screen

Opening the app lands you on your clipboard history, shown as a grid of
cards. Each card is one clip (text, image, or a single file), newest
first. The top bar has three things:

- **Search** — filter history by content.
- **Current server name** — tap it to open the switcher and move between
  multiple desktops (see [Connect to a Desktop](./connect)).
- **Connection status dot** — shows whether your link to the desktop is
  healthy.

You can filter history by type (text / image / file) to see only what you
care about.

## Adding content

The **Add** floating action button (FAB) in the bottom-right corner
expands into 5 actions:

| Action           | What it does                                        |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Take photo       | Shoot a new photo and upload it                     |
| Pick image       | Upload an existing image from your photo library    |
| Pick file        | Upload a file                                       |
| Upload clipboard | Upload whatever is on your phone's system clipboard |
| Sync now         | Do one send/receive with the desktop immediately    |

After you pick something, it appears in your history right away (see
[Saved means done](#saved-means-done-and-the-clock-badge) below), then
gets pushed to the desktop in the background.

## Uploading via the system share sheet

You don't have to open UniClipboard first. From **any** app, tap the
system share button and choose **UniClipboard** from the share sheet to
send the current item (text, link, image, file) over.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Share from the source app

In your browser, photos, files, or any other app, tap the system share
button and pick UniClipboard.

</Step>

<Step>

### It lands in local history instantly

The shared content is written to UniClipboard's history immediately — no
waiting on the network.

</Step>

<Step>

### You return to the source app

The share sheet closes and you go back to what you were doing. Pushing to
the desktop happens in the background and doesn't cost you any time. (In
some cases, such as screenshots and system shares, the UI may stay inside
the app.)

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  On iOS, the share extension appears in the system share sheet automatically once the app is
  installed — you don't need to enable it in Settings. If it's ranked low, you can drag it forward
  manually and mark it as a favorite in the share sheet. See [iOS Extensions](./ios-extensions).
</Callout>

## Saved means done, and the clock badge

Whether you use the FAB or the system share sheet, UniClipboard always
**saves locally first and pushes in the background**: the moment content
enters your history it's done, and you can see it right away.

A card that hasn't been pushed successfully yet shows a **clock icon** in
its bottom-right corner, meaning "pending upload / pending sync." The
background retries automatically, and once the push to the desktop
succeeds the badge disappears. So a clock you see is nothing to act on —
it clears itself. Only a clock that never goes away signals a connection
problem (see [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting)).

## Foreground auto-sync

The main screen has an auto-sync toggle. When it's on, as long as the app
is in the foreground it does two things at once:

- **Auto-apply remote** — new clips on the desktop flow into your phone
  automatically.
- **Auto-push local** — anything you newly copy on the phone is sent to
  the desktop automatically.

<Callout type="info">
  This foreground toggle only works while the app is open. To send and receive even when the app
  isn't in the foreground or the screen is locked, iOS relies on the keyboard / share extensions
  plus background configuration, and Android relies on background clipboard access — see [iOS
  Extensions](./ios-extensions) and [Android Background Access](./android-access) respectively.
</Callout>

## Card detail actions

Tap any card to open its detail view, where you can:

- **Copy** — write this content back to your phone's system clipboard.
- **Save image to library** — for image content.
- **Save file** — for file content.
- **Delete** — remove this entry from history.

## Supported content types

The UniClipboard mobile app syncs three kinds of content:

- **Text** (including links)
- **Images**
- **A single file**
