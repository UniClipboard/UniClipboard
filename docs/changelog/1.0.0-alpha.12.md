## 1.0.0-alpha.12 - 2026-09-15

### Features

- Automatically back up local data before a data upgrade, show backup progress, and let you view or delete backups in Storage settings (#1671)
- Reset your space passphrase without losing local history when your device is unlocked and is the only device in the space, invalidating existing invitations (#1672)
- Show when pairing is awaiting confirmation or remains unconfirmed, and explain when a join attempt expires or is replaced by a newer pairing (#1675)

### Fixes

- Accept passphrases with leading or trailing spaces when confirming your passphrase to add a device (#1672)
