## 1.0.0-alpha.9 - 2026-09-10

### Features

- Turn sync on or off for all devices or individual paired devices directly from the tray menu (#1638)
- Refresh device connections from the Devices page and see a summary of the results (#1641)
- Retry startup, check for updates, or contact support directly from the startup failure screen (#1643)
- Choose automatic, app, system, or no title bar on Windows and Linux, with your choice respected from startup (#1646)
- See progress and elapsed time while your local data is upgraded before the app opens (#1647)

### Fixes

- Fix text and icon alignment in single-line notifications (#1641)
- Fix startup failures when upgrading older local data, including data from alpha.5 and interrupted upgrades (#1647)
- Restore missing minimize, maximize, and close buttons when using the app title bar on Windows and Linux (#1648)
