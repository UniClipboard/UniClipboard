## v2.0.0-build.13 (desktop) - 2026-11-26

### Features

- The desktop app is now consistently branded as Unic across its window, system integration, and installer. (#2404)
- Pairing screens now show QR codes for pairing codes and full pairing tickets, making it faster to connect another device. (#2403)
- Sync transfers now show progress and delivery status in history, while incoming file transfers show progress and can be discarded from the notification. (#2402)
- Search now offers autocomplete for tags, types, devices, dates, and file extensions, with a favorites-only filter and improved keyboard controls in the Quick Panel. (#2412)
- Windows users can choose whether the Quick Panel opens in the center of the screen, near the mouse pointer, or beside the text cursor. (#2413)

### Fixes

- Reopening the Quick Panel now selects the first result instead of restoring a previous selection. (#2411)
- Changing sync policies or settings no longer causes the entire page to flicker. (#2406)
