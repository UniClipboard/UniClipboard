## v3.0.0-build.206 (ios) - 2026-11-27

### Features

- A new iOS app experience makes it easier to browse, search, copy, and send synced clipboard history, including text and images. (#2343)
- New onboarding guides you through pairing, system permissions, and iOS sharing and keyboard features. (#2426)
- Pairing is easier with QR-code scanning, clearer device approval, and native iOS sheets for pairing and device management. (#2384) (#2386) (#2430)
- Existing UniClipboard mobile history can be imported automatically during setup, with favorites preserved. (#2410) (#2432)
- Share content to paired devices from the iOS Share Extension, and use the same sending flow from history. (#2385) (#2391)
- The iOS Keyboard Extension shows synced clips and can send selected clips to your devices, with optional live clipboard sync while the keyboard is open. (#2389) (#2436)
- Opening the app can show a native iOS prompt when clipboard access is needed to refresh local history. (#2392)

### Fixes

- Improved iOS screens, sheets, device settings, and history interactions to better match familiar Apple conventions. (#2377) (#2382)
